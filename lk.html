Loshn-Koydesh Vowel Length (experimental)

For words taken from Hebrew/Aramaic. Step 1: get a pointed spelling. Step 2: confirm it. Step 3: apply the rules.

1. Enter the word

Nakdan (Dicta) points the word the way Hebrew is normally pointed. It may not match the Yiddish spelling, so the next step checks it.

2. Is this spelling correct?

You can edit the spelling above (for example to fix a vowel or a dagesh) before confirming.

Look the word up in one of these, then correct the spelling above and press “Yes”:

Morfix Milog

3. Apply the rules

Confirmed spelling:

The app reads the pointing as Ashkenazic Yiddish sounds (patah = a, qamats = o, segol = e, tsere = ey, holam = oy, hiriq = i, shuruk/qubuts = u) and guesses the stress. Check both, since they decide the result.
